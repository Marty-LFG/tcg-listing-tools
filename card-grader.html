Card Pre-Grader — before you pay the fee

PSA · BGS · CGC · PCG · TAG

Scan or photograph the card and microscope its corners. You get measured centering, an AI condition pass, a predicted grade at each company, the value at that grade, and a straight answer on whether it's worth sending.

An estimate, not a grade. This screens cards, and it's much better at telling you a card isn't a 10 than proving one is. Surface flaws and the back are hard to see from images, and it doesn't authenticate anything. Treat every number here as a guide, especially around the 9-vs-10 line.
